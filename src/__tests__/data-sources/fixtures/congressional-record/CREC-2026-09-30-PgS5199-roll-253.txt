                             CLOTURE MOTION

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Pursuant to rule XXII, the Chair lays before 
the Senate the pending cloture motion, which the clerk will state.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the motion to 
     proceed to Calendar No. 548, H.R. 7008, a bill to amend 
     chapter 131 of title 5 to require certain restrictions on 
     stocks for Members of Congress and their spouses and 
     dependents, and for other purposes.
         John Thune, Tim Sheehy, Rick Scott of Florida, Ted Cruz, 
           Ashley B. Moody, Steve Daines, Cynthia M. Lummis, John 
           R. Curtis, Pete Ricketts, Bernie Moreno, John Barrasso, 
           Eric Schmitt, Jon A. Husted, Mike Crapo, David 
           McCormick, Katie Boyd Britt, John Cornyn.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. By unanimous consent, the mandatory quorum 
call has been waived.
  The question is, Is it the sense of the Senate that debate on the 
motion to proceed to Calendar No. 548, H.R. 7008, a bill to amend 
chapter 131 of title 5 to require certain restrictions on stocks for 
Members of Congress and their spouses and dependents, and for other 
purposes, shall be brought to a close?
  The yeas and nays are mandatory under the rule.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk called the roll.
  The yeas and nays resulted--yeas 53, nays 47, as follows:
