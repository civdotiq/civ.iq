Measures Passed:
  National Park System:  Senate passed S. 2498, to authorize the 
Secretary of the Interior to extend certain leases within units of the 
National Park System without opening the lease to bidding, after 
agreeing to the committee amendment in the nature of a substitute. 
                                                         Pages S5201-03
  Crystal Reservoir Conveyance Act:  Senate passed S. 2754, to direct 
the Secretary of Agriculture to convey to the City of Ouray, Colorado, 
certain land managed by the Forest Service, together with a reservoir, 
after agreeing to the committee amendment in the nature of a 
substitute.
  Pages S5201-03
  Safe Access to Cash Act:  Senate passed S. 3798, to amend title 18, 
United States Code, to establish criminal offenses with respect to 
violations involving ATMs, regardless of whether the ATM is located on 
the physical premises of a financial institution, after agreeing to the 
committee amendment in the nature of a substitute.
  Page S5207
  Taxpayer Assistance and Service Act:  Committee on Finance was 
discharged from further consideration of H.R. 5349, to improve services 
provided to taxpayers by the Internal Revenue Service, and the bill was 
then passed, after agreeing to the following amendments proposed 
thereto:

the Chiricahua National Monument in the State of Arizona as Chiricahua 
National Park, and the bill was then passed.
  Page S5226

of the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency to coordinate 
to improve cybersecurity in the health care and public health sectors, 
after withdrawing the committee amendment in the nature of a 
substitute, and agreeing to the following amendment proposed thereto: 
                                                         Pages S5231-36
  Cassidy Amendment No. 5819, in the nature of a substitute.

Transportation was discharged from further consideration of H.R. 7022, 
to provide for the transmission of emergency alerts by satellite, and 
the bill was then passed.
  Pages S5237-38
  End Tax Penalties on American Hostages Act:  Committee on Finance was 
discharged from further consideration of H.R. 9496, to amend the 
Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to postpone tax deadlines and reimburse 
paid late fees for United States nationals who are unlawfully or 
wrongfully detained or held hostage abroad, and the bill was then 
passed.
  Pages S5238-39
  Wyoming Education Trust Modernization Act:  Senate passed S. 2273, to 
amend the Act of July 10, 1890, to modify certain provisions relating 
to the disposal of public land in the State of Wyoming for educational 
purposes.
